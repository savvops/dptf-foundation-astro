## 🎯 Major Foundation Activities Identified

### 1. Medical Outreach Programs
- **Dec 2020**: First Medical Outreach at Atighir Village, Tarka LGA, Benue State
  - Treated 1,053+ patients
  - 2-day free medical service
  - Drugs distribution
  - COVID-19 protocols observed
  
- **Nov 2023**: Mini Medical Outreach at Our Lady of Fatima Girls Secondary School, Kaduna
  - Health symposium
  - Medical checkups for students
  - Award received from school

- **Sept 2024**: Medical Intervention at St. Gerard Catholic Hospital, Kakuri, Kaduna
  - 1-week accident & emergency support
  - Supplied medical consumables
  - Partnership with Veritas University Teaching Hospital

### 2. Scholarship & Educational Support
- **BSU Prize**: Best Graduating Female Medical Doctor at Benue State University
  - Started 2020/2021 academic session
  - Prize: ₦300,000 bank draft presented
  - Convocation attendance Dec 7, 2024

- **Educational Materials Donation** (June 2021)
  - Donated to Kadwell International School, Barnawa
  - Books and learning materials

- **Scholarship Program** (2023)
  - Call for applications for 2023 scholarship

### 3. Food Security & Charity Programs
- **Operation Feed the North** / **Operation Feed AMAC**
  - Food items distribution program
  - Partnership with AHEF NGO
  - Items: Rice, beans, garri, oil, indomie, spaghetti, tomatoes, onions
  - Target: 1,000 beneficiaries

### 4. Annual Memorial Activities
- **Thanksgiving Mass** held annually on Sept 15/17
  - Holy Family Catholic Church, Barnawa
  - Annual memorial of Dr. Patience's passing
